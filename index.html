About Me

I began my professional career in the mortgage industry at Scotiabank, where I was proud to receive the "Best-of-the-Best" award for my dedication and performance. I later transitioned into an Operations Analyst role, managing multiple equity and fixed income books, as well as overseeing security setups.

I then joined Peoples Group as a Portfolio Specialist, handling large portfolio transactions and developing custom Excel macros to automate repetitive tasks — saving the team countless hours. I streamlined data processing, report generation, and data validation using advanced VBA scripting, and even automated personalized email generation to improve team communication.

This hands-on technical expertise led to my promotion to Reporting Analyst, where I currently consolidate and clean partner files, build VBA tools for cross-team automation, and write Fiserv DNA queries to manage large volumes of deposit data. I also create visually impactful dashboards and reports to support strategic decision-making across the business.
